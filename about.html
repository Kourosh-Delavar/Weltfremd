About

Weltfremd is a space dedicated to the deliberate exploration of art, philosophy, and technology, and the underlying relations that bind them, Guided by the principle "One word by many, many words by one".

Founded by K. Delavar, who is also the publication's first author, the site was built to house thoughts that venture beyond the conventional boundaries of rationality and irrationality. It is a place for free thinkers to express the full extent of their intellectual freedom.

New essays are published irregularly, arriving only when they are ready.
